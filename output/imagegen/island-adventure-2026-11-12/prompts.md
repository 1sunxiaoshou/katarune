## 01-first-island

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one landscape 16:9 in-game screenshot concept of an original 3D otherworldly island adventure RPG with an embodied AI companion. Simulated gameplay visual, not an existing shipped game.
Art direction: A unified NON-REALISTIC anime 3D videogame art direction for EVERYTHING: terrain, foliage, architecture, water, sky, props, creatures and characters. Clean delicate linework, flat-color cel shading with only two or three large soft shadow bands, intentionally simplified geometry with elegant silhouettes, saturated yet harmonious colors, painterly-designed color-block foliage and clouds but NO visible brush strokes. Stylized ocean made of turquoise and cobalt color planes with graphic white foam curls; faceted simplified rocks; broad designed leaf clusters rather than individual photoreal leaves. Convincing polished playable real-time 3D space and coherent perspective. No PBR look, no photographic textures, no microdetail, no physically realistic rendering, no realistic water, no skin pores, no gritty rock textures, no depth-of-field blur. Characters and environment belong to the exact SAME anime cel-shaded world, not anime characters composited into realistic scenery. Maintain full-size adventure-game proportions; NOT chibi, not voxel, not low-poly toy diorama. Full color, clear spatial depth, strong silhouettes. A wondrous inhabited fantasy ocean world, inviting exploration, survival, crafting, sailing and making a home together. Magical ecology must be concrete and physically present, not generic glowing particles everywhere.

Female AI companion with silver-gray shoulder-length hair, teal eyes, ivory expedition jacket over muted teal clothing, dark shorts over opaque leggings, boots and small crossbody bag. Same designs throughout. Both are actual full 3D figures integrated with ground, perspective, lighting and object interaction; elegant adult anime proportions, no chibi, no pinup. Player and companion each doing something useful.
Gameplay presentation: clear third-person playable camera, medium-wide frame emphasizing the place and reachable objective, not a promotional poster or cinematic portrait.
